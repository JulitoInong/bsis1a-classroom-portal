BSIS 1-A — FINAL ACCOUNT SWITCH VERIFICATION PLAN

Static automated checks in this package validate the production-safe account-switch implementation.
Manual live verification remains required for the real Google account chooser and Supabase session.

Manual sequence:
1. Sign in with Google account A.
2. Reach Registrar.
3. Click Use a different Google account.
4. Confirm login page remains visible and does not auto-redirect.
5. Click Sign in with Google.
6. Confirm Google displays the account chooser.
7. Choose account B.
8. Confirm Registrar identifies account B.
